## ROI and image operations

Consume validated RGB uint8 THW3 plus protection/motion/cuts from source preparation. Use semantic protection >=.5 or motion >=.5, explicitly including all P=1 pixels. Accumulate per scene segment, then derive connected-component bounding boxes with a context margin .05 of min(H,W). OD is one frame. Empty support yields no ROI candidates. Use a closing kernel derived from .05 of min(H,W), preserve the ROI union, low-pass the interior closure envelope, and gray the exterior. FIR: 25-tap normalized Lanczos-windowed sinc, cutoff .1; this is a scaled adaptation, not bit-exact reproduction of the paper's bandwidth .01 implementation. Apply common (1-P)*(1-.75M) blend with strengths .5/1 and restore P=1 exactly before codec.

Retarget presets have background downsample factors 2/4, ROI factor 1. Build axis edges from source boundaries and ROI box edges. An interval crossing any ROI is retained; unprotected intervals shrink. This implements the rectangular-grid min-over-row/column rule for uniform ROI factor 1. Each source segment has a fixed grid; all segments share the largest retarget width/height, rounded up to even and capped by original dimensions. Resize individual rectangular cells with AREA for shrinking and LINEAR for enlargement. Source dimension and frame count remain fixed after inverse retarget. Identical/no-gain grids are omitted. Temporal sampling/frame counts/FPS are not changed by V32.

## Transport and codec

A versioned binary VC32 packet carries codec/task, source and coded geometry, frame count, rational coded FPS, segment count, mapping length, stream length and integrity checksum. Metadata has source/destination axis edges and segment ranges, with strict monotonic integer validation, geometry bounds, exact coverage, limits and no trailing bytes. Identity/plain controls also carry the common header. Plain AR resize controls retain their historical coded geometry; retarget is inversely mapped to source geometry. The receiver consumes only packet bytes, never source-side masks/grids, and runs the standard strict FFmpeg decoder followed by inverse retarget as needed.

Report elementary bytes, metadata/header bytes and total packet bytes. Selection/BD-rate use total bytes and original-source-pixel denominator. Verify the emitted packet with an independent parse/decode; do not merely inverse-map with in-memory encoder metadata. Cache identical coded RGB for encoding, but include mapping/packet identity in reconstruction and teacher cache. Required metadata is never free or counted twice. Systematic codec errors abort; do not hide them behind identity.
